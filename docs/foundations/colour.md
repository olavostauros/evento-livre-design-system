# Colour

Implements `docs/decisions/0001-colour-palette.md` (two hues, one per
register, brand deep teal-blue). Values live in `src/tokens/colors.ts`;
this page explains how to use them and proves the contrast claims.

Everything here is temporary in the sense of 0001: hues may be swapped by
editing the recipe in `scripts/build-palette.ts` and running
`bun run build:palette`. Names, roles and the contrast rules do not change
when hues do.

## Three layers

1. **Palette.** Eight scales, eleven steps each (50 lightest to 950
   darkest). Named by role: `brand`, `accent-b2b`, `accent-b2c`, `neutral`,
   `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info`. Tailwind: `bg-brand-600`.
2. **Roles.** What a colour is *for*: `canvas`, `surface`, `fg`, `border`,
   `ring`, `link`, `brand-solid`, `status-danger-fg`, and so on. Roles
   resolve to a palette step per theme. Tailwind: `bg-canvas`,
   `text-fg-muted`, `border-border-strong`.
3. **Register.** `accent-*` is an alias that points at `accent-b2c-*` by
   default and at `accent-b2b-*` inside any `data-register="b2b"` ancestor.
   Tailwind: `bg-accent-500`.

Components use roles for surfaces, text and borders, `accent-*` for
register-specific emphasis, and reach into the palette only for the brand
and status scales. Tailwind's default colours are removed from the theme, so
`bg-red-500` does not exist; a colour that is not a token cannot be used.

## Themes and registers

- Light is the default. Dark follows `prefers-color-scheme` unless
  `data-theme="light"` or `data-theme="dark"` is set on an ancestor
  (usually `<html>`). Dark is required by 0001: attendees use the product at
  night.
- Register defaults to `b2c`. Producer surfaces set `data-register="b2b"`
  on their root. Only the accent changes; brand, neutrals and status are
  shared, so both registers read as one company.

## How the scales were built

One lightness ramp and one chroma curve in OKLCH, applied to every hue, so
every scale behaves the same way:

| Step | Role in the system |
|---|---|
| 50, 100 | Tinted backgrounds (status banners, selected rows) |
| 200, 300 | Borders on tinted backgrounds; dark-theme text and icons at 300 |
| 400 | Dark-theme text at AA on the dark canvas; light-theme decorative only |
| 500 | Focus rings and UI boundaries (3:1 on white); never body text on white |
| 600 | First step that carries white text at AA. Solid buttons in both themes |
| 700 | Light-theme text and icons at AA on canvas and surface |
| 800 to 950 | Dark-theme tinted backgrounds and borders; deep fills |

Hues sit at least 35 degrees apart in OKLCH so every pair stays apart,
including pairs that colour-vision deficiency compresses. Two collisions
were resolved deliberately:

- **Info is purple, not blue.** A blue info scale would sit on the brand.
- **Danger is orange-red.** It leans away from the hot-pink B2C accent.

Success is green at 150 degrees, 68 degrees from the brand, so the two are
distinguishable at every step (0001 amendment). A test in
`src/tokens/colors.test.ts` enforces this and every contrast claim below.

## Roles per theme

| Role | Light | Dark | Guarantee |
|---|---|---|---|
| canvas | neutral-50 | neutral-950 | Page background |
| surface | white | neutral-900 | Cards, inputs, sheets |
| surface-raised | white | neutral-800 | Popovers, menus |
| surface-overlay | white | neutral-800 | Dialogs |
| surface-sunken | neutral-100 | black | Wells, table headers |
| fg | neutral-950 | neutral-50 | AA on every surface |
| fg-muted | neutral-700 | neutral-200 | AA on every surface |
| fg-subtle | neutral-600 | neutral-300 | AA on canvas and surface |
| fg-inverse | white | white | AA on brand-solid and every status solid |
| border | neutral-200 | neutral-800 | Decorative hairline, no contrast claim |
| border-strong | neutral-500 | neutral-500 | 3:1 on canvas and surface (inputs) |
| ring | brand-500 | brand-400 | 3:1 on canvas and surface |
| link | brand-700 | brand-300 | AA on canvas and surface |
| brand-solid | brand-600 | brand-600 | Carries fg-inverse at AA |
| brand-fg | brand-700 | brand-300 | AA on canvas, surface and brand-bg |
| brand-bg | brand-50 | brand-950 | Tint for selection and emphasis |
| status-*-fg | *-700 | *-300 | AA on status-*-bg, canvas and surface |
| status-*-bg | *-50 | *-950 | Banner and badge tint |
| status-*-border | *-200 | *-800 | Border for the tint |
| status-*-solid | *-600 | *-600 | Carries fg-inverse at AA |

Dark-theme elevation lightens the surface per level (0006). Surfaces stop
at neutral-800 because `fg-subtle` loses AA on anything lighter; the
overlay level relies on a border and a scrim, not a lighter fill.

## Rules

- Never hardcode a hex value in a component. If a colour is missing, add a
  role or a palette step in `src/tokens/colors.ts` and rebuild the theme.
- Text on a colour must be `fg-inverse` on a `*-solid` role, or a `*-fg`
  role on `*-bg`, canvas or surface. Do not put text on steps 400 or 500.
- The brand hue is for primary actions, focus, links and the logotype.
  The accent is for emphasis, highlights and data, never for primary
  actions.
- Status colour is never the only signal. Pair it with an icon or text.
- Event artwork sits next to the brand constantly. Keep large fills neutral
  so the artwork stays the hero; brand and accent are small-area colours.
